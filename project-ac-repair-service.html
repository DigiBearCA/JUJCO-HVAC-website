Cooling project

Emergency AC Repair

Home/Projects/AC repair

A failed capacitor and tired contactor took cooling out on a 32°C afternoon. Truck stock, house cool again that day.

The outdoor fan hummed and the air inside stayed at 27°C. Amp draw said the compressor was still viable. The capacitor was swollen and the contactor face was pitted.

Both parts came off the truck. We cleared a sluggish condensate drain that would have been the next trip, then confirmed the temperature split.

The homeowner had cool bedrooms by late afternoon and a written note that the condenser is late in life — repair this year, plan a replacement before the next heat wave.

The hard part

Same-day parts during a city-wide heat wave.

What they have now

Cooling restored without a second visit, plus a clear replacement horizon.
