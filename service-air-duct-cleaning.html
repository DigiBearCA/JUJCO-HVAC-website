Airflow · Dust

Clean trunks, real airflow

After a renovation, a long-overdue filter habit, or a decade of pet hair, the main trunks hold a surprising amount of grit. That grit becomes the dust on your baseboards every time the furnace kicks on.

We put the system under negative pressure, agitate the runs, clean the blower compartment, and wipe the registers. If a run is crushed or disconnected in a joist bay, we tell you - cleaning will not fix a duct that is not attached.

Questions we actually get

How often should ducts be cleaned?

Not every year. After renovations, a long dust problem, or a decade is a better rule than a calendar scare.

Will this fix a weak bedroom?

If the run is full of debris, yes. If the run is undersized or crushed, you need duct repair, not perfume.

Do you use chemicals?

No fogging as a substitute for removal. If you want a sanitizer after a specific issue, we talk about that separately.
