Heating and Cooling · Cold Climate

Heat pumps engineered for Alberta winter conditions

Standard heat pumps that struggle when temperatures drop are not suitable for Alberta winters. We install cold climate equipment engineered to deliver dependable heat in subzero Canadian temperatures. Our technicians evaluate your existing setup to determine whether a standalone heat pump or a hybrid dual fuel system paired with your gas furnace is the most reliable choice for your home.

Proper equipment placement, dedicated refrigerant line sets, electrical safety, and accurate defrost calibration ensure your system runs smoothly through every season. We provide complete installations and prompt diagnostic repairs for existing units, including compressor service, defrost sensor calibration, and auxiliary heat lockout adjustments. We also guide you through thermostat controls so your transition between heating and cooling is effortless.

Frequently asked questions

Do heat pumps work in Alberta cold winters?

Modern cold climate heat pumps continue providing efficient warmth down to subzero temperatures between minus 25 and minus 30 degrees Celsius. Across Alberta, most homes pair their heat pump with an existing gas furnace in a dual fuel hybrid setup. The heat pump handles heating during moderate winter weather and summer cooling, while the gas furnace automatically turns on during extreme cold snaps.

How much does a heat pump cost in Alberta?

Fully installed ducted heat pump systems in Alberta generally range from $6,000 to $14,000, while complete dual fuel hybrid systems paired with a new furnace range from $10,000 to $18,000. Total costs depend on equipment heating capacity, electrical panel capability, ductwork integration, and whether single zone or whole home heating is selected.

Can I keep my existing furnace with a heat pump?

Yes, keeping your current furnace is one of the most effective and popular heating choices across Alberta. In a dual fuel hybrid setup, an indoor evaporator coil is added above your furnace, allowing the electric heat pump to provide efficient primary heat and cooling, while your reliable gas furnace serves as auxiliary backup during deep freezes.

Are rebates and financing available for heat pumps in Alberta?

Homeowners can take advantage of interest free financing through federal loan initiatives as well as municipal programs such as the Clean Energy Improvement Program in participating Alberta communities. Additionally, manufacturers frequently offer seasonal equipment rebates. Our team assists you with all required documentation to maximize available savings.
