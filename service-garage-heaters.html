Garage Heaters Installation & Repair

Home/Services/Garage Heaters

Turn your freezing Alberta garage into a warm and comfortable workspace all winter with a professionally installed and properly sized garage heater.

Heating · Alberta

Engineered for harsh Alberta winters and freezing temperatures

Garages across Alberta lose heat quickly through concrete floors, overhead doors, and uninsulated framing. An undersized unit will run constantly without keeping the space comfortable, while an oversized heater will cycle repeatedly and waste fuel. We assess your garage dimensions, ceiling height, door insulation, and electrical capacity to install the ideal heating solution for your needs.

Our certified technicians install forced air gas unit heaters and radiant tube heaters with precision. We safely mount the equipment to ceiling joists, connect dedicated gas lines, install exterior exhaust venting that meets all safety codes, and wire a responsive wall thermostat. Before we complete the job, we test gas pressure, verify flame combustion, and check temperature output so your garage remains warm all season.

How we do it

The visit, step by step

1

Assess your garage

We calculate heating load based on garage dimensions, ceiling height, door construction, and insulation.

2

Mount and position

Frequently asked questions

Which is better, a forced air unit heater or a radiant tube heater?

Forced air unit heaters warm the air quickly, which is ideal if you open vehicle doors frequently or need fast heat. Radiant tube heaters warm concrete slabs, workbenches, and tools directly without blowing air or raising dust, making them remarkably quiet and comfortable for long shop projects. We inspect your layout to help you select the ideal option.

How long does a garage heater installation take?

Most standard installations take a single day. When replacing an existing garage heater, our technicians often complete the work within four to six hours. If your project involves extending a new gas line from the house or drilling through exterior walls for venting, we provide an exact schedule before starting.

Can you install a heater in an uninsulated garage?

Yes, though quality insulation substantially reduces ongoing heating costs. If your garage has uninsulated walls or thin metal doors, heat escapes rapidly. We calculate the exact heat requirement so the unit can maintain warmth, and we provide straightforward tips on insulation and door weatherstripping to help you conserve energy.

What routine maintenance does a garage heater require?

Because garages accumulate dust, sawdust, and vehicle exhaust, heaters benefit from an annual inspection before winter arrives. A standard check includes cleaning the burner assembly, testing the flame sensor, checking safety shutoff switches, inspecting exhaust venting for blockages, and verifying optimal gas pressure.
