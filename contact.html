Helpful Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can a technician respond to emergency heating calls in Alberta?

Our emergency response line is monitored 24 hours a day, seven days a week. When extreme subzero temperatures hit Alberta, heating failures require immediate intervention to prevent frozen pipes and protect family safety. We prioritize urgent heating outages and dispatch experienced technicians promptly to restore warmth to your home.

Do you provide free estimates for new equipment installations?

Yes. We offer complimentary, no obligation consultations for new furnace installations, heat pump additions, air conditioning systems, and garage heaters across Alberta. A qualified specialist will assess your property, calculate required heating loads, and provide clear written pricing options tailored to your comfort goals and budget.

What regions across Alberta do you service?

JUJCO proudly serves residential homeowners and commercial property managers throughout Alberta. Our primary service hubs include Edmonton, Calgary, Red Deer, Sherwood Park, St. Albert, Spruce Grove, Stony Plain, Leduc, Beaumont, Fort Saskatchewan, and adjacent Alberta communities. If you are unsure whether your location falls within our service zone, contact our office and we will gladly assist you.

What basic checks should I perform before booking a service call?

Before scheduling a service visit, we recommend checking that your thermostat has fresh batteries and is set to the correct mode, verifying that your furnace power switch is turned on, checking the main electrical breaker panel, and inspecting your furnace air filter for heavy dust accumulation. If these basic checks do not restore operation, our technicians are ready to diagnose and resolve the issue quickly.
