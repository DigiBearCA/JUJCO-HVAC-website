Seasonal Tune Ups · Alberta

Proactive inspections before severe weather arrives

Most heating emergencies our technicians handle in deep winter began as minor issues that could easily have been caught in the fall, such as a weakened igniter, a carbon coated flame sensor, a clogged condensate trap, or failing blower bearings. An annual tune up means thoroughly inspecting internal mechanical and electrical parts before harsh weather puts peak strain on your equipment.

Spring air conditioning maintenance follows the same principle. Outdoor capacitors, fan motors, contactor points, and refrigerant lines frequently fail during the initial summer heat wave if left unserviced. We measure and record electrical amp draws, combustion efficiency, and temperature drops so your equipment maintains a dependable operating record year after year.

Frequently asked questions

How often should a furnace and air conditioner be serviced in Alberta?

In Alberta, heating and cooling equipment should receive professional service once per year for each system. Furnaces should be tuned up in the autumn between September and November before freezing winter temperatures arrive. Central air conditioners and heat pumps should be serviced in the spring between April and June before summer heat begins. Regular seasonal service ensures equipment runs reliably when you need it most.

What is included in a professional furnace tune up?

A complete furnace tune up includes cleaning the burners and flame sensor, testing the hot surface igniter, inspecting the heat exchanger for cracks or rust, checking electrical capacitor values and motor amp draw, testing safety limit switches, verifying gas manifold pressure, flushing condensate lines on high efficiency units, and testing carbon monoxide levels and combustion safety.

Does regular HVAC maintenance really lower heating and cooling bills in Alberta?

Yes. Dust buildup on furnace blowers, dirty burners, and debris clogged outdoor condenser coils force equipment to work harder and cycle longer, increasing natural gas and electrical consumption by 10% to 15%. Routine maintenance restores optimal airflow and combustion efficiency, helping your systems run at peak performance while reducing monthly utility costs throughout severe Alberta winters and hot summers.

Will skipping annual maintenance void my equipment warranty?

Most major HVAC equipment manufacturers require documented annual professional maintenance to keep their parts and heat exchanger warranties valid. If an unserviced furnace or compressor suffers a major failure, manufacturers may decline warranty coverage for replacement parts. JUJCO provides detailed written inspection reports after every visit, giving you the proof of service needed to protect your warranty.
