Why a Spring HVAC Tune-Up Pays Off

Home/Blog/Maintenance guide

Spring is when we can still get to you. July is when everyone else calls too.

2 April 2025 · JUJCO Heating & Cooling

Spring is when we can still get to you. July is when everyone else calls too.

Capacitors age in the dark

They sit all winter and fail when you first ask for cooling. A meter in April tells you.

Drains clog after a dusty winter

A blocked condensate line is a wet ceiling and a shutdown on the first humid day.

Coils do not clean themselves

A dirty outdoor coil raises head pressure and your bill. Rinsing it is part of a real tune-up.

You get a baseline

Temperature split and amp draw written down this spring make next year’s visit a comparison, not a guess.

The furnace still matters

Shoulder season is when we catch the ignitor that will not survive October. Ask us to look at both.

Next step

Book the spring slot before the first 28°C forecast.
