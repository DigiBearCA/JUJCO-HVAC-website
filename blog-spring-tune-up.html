Why a Spring HVAC Tune Up Pays Off

Home/Blog/Maintenance guide

Scheduling your cooling inspection early in spring ensures dependable comfort before warm Alberta summer temperatures arrive.

02 Apr, 2025 · JUJCO Heating & Cooling

As winter snow melts across Alberta, heating systems transition into cooling season. While it can be tempting to wait until the first humid summer afternoon to turn on your air conditioner, proactive spring maintenance is the most dependable way to avoid unexpected breakdowns. Investing in a professional tune up keeps your equipment running smoothly, extends system lifespan, and lowers monthly energy bills.

Prevent unexpected summer breakdowns

Air conditioner run capacitors and electrical contactors experience significant wear throughout fluctuating seasonal cycles. After sitting dormant all winter, these components can fail the moment cooling demand spikes. Our technicians measure electrical microfarad ratings and contact resistance during a spring inspection, catching failing parts before you lose cooling on a scorching day.

Clear winter debris and clean condenser coils

Over months of harsh Alberta weather, outdoor condenser units collect leaves, dirt, dust, and windblown debris. Restricted airflow forces the compressor to work harder, driving up power consumption and creating elevated operating temperatures. Thoroughly rinsing and clearing the outdoor coils restores proper heat transfer and protects the compressor against premature failure.

Inspect and clear condensate drain lines

Your air conditioner removes substantial humidity from indoor air as it cools. That moisture drains through a dedicated condensate line. Dust, algae, and airborne particles can accumulate inside the drain line during shoulder seasons, leading to clogs that trigger water leaks, ceiling damage, or safety float switch shutdowns. Clearing the drain ensures smooth, trouble free operation.

Establish baseline performance metrics

Every comprehensive tune up includes measuring key system data such as temperature drop across the coil, refrigerant line temperatures, and motor electrical amperage. Recording these operating metrics provides a clear benchmark for your system. If performance shifts over time, our team can pinpoint minor efficiency drops before they turn into costly mechanical repairs.

Check furnace readiness for the next heating cycle

Spring is also an ideal time to assess your furnace following months of continuous winter operation. Inspecting the blower wheel, burner assembly, and hot surface ignitor during the same visit ensures that your heating system will be ready when temperatures drop again in autumn. Combining cooling and heating checks saves time and provides total peace of mind.
