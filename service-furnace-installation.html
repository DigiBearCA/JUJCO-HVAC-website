Heating · Alberta

Built for severe winter temperatures across Alberta

Heating systems in Alberta work under demanding winter conditions. Furnaces often struggle when they are improperly sized, cycling continuously, or operating with outdated venting. We begin with a comprehensive heat loss assessment of your home, examining insulation, window placement, and duct airflow. We then recommend an advanced two stage or modulating gas furnace that maintains consistent warmth efficiently.

Our technicians safely remove the existing equipment and upgrade gas lines, intake piping, exhaust venting, and condensate drainage to current safety codes. Once installed, we test combustion performance, calibrate blower airflow for your ductwork, and guide you through thermostat operation and filter maintenance. Whether you need a full furnace replacement or precise diagnostic heating repairs, our team delivers dependable comfort.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a new furnace cost in Alberta?

Standard furnace replacements in Alberta typically range from $4,000 to $7,500 depending on home size, multi stage heating efficiency, and mechanical room configuration. Transparent quotes include complete installation, removal of old equipment, safety permits, and full parts and labour warranties.

How long does a furnace typically last in Alberta?

Because of demanding subzero winter conditions, a quality furnace in Alberta generally lasts 15 to 20 years with annual maintenance. Routine filter replacements and seasonal inspections protect critical components and prevent sudden breakdowns during cold snaps.

Should I repair or replace my aging furnace?

If your furnace is over 15 years old and repair costs approach half the price of a new system, replacement is usually the best financial decision. Frequent cycling issues, rising heating bills, or a cracked heat exchanger indicate that a new high efficiency furnace will deliver safer and more economical warmth.

Are government rebates available for new furnaces in Alberta?

Qualifying high efficiency heating systems may be eligible for municipal, provincial, or energy efficiency grant programs across Alberta. Our team provides verified equipment documentation and assists with paperwork to help you secure all eligible savings.
