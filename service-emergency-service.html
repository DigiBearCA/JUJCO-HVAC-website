24/7 Emergency Service · Alberta

Rapid response when your heating or cooling system fails

Our standard office line handles scheduled estimates and regular weekday appointments, while our dedicated emergency line operates 24 hours a day for urgent situations. When you face a total loss of heat at minus 28°C, a sounding carbon monoxide detector, a persistent gas odour, or air conditioning failure during an extreme heat advisory, our certified technicians respond promptly. We travel in fully equipped service vehicles carrying universal igniters, flame sensors, run capacitors, blower motors, and control boards so urgent repairs can be completed during the initial visit.

If an unusual manufacturer component requires ordering from local suppliers, we ensure your building remains safe and protected against freezing pipes. We provide space heating options, execute safe system shutdowns when necessary, and schedule the earliest priority morning repair. You will never be left uncertain about the status of your heating or cooling system.

Frequently asked questions

What is considered an HVAC emergency in Alberta?

An HVAC emergency includes a complete heating failure during subzero winter temperatures, a sounding carbon monoxide alarm, a strong smell of natural gas, active water flooding from a boiler or cracked plumbing lines, electrical burning smells from equipment, or total loss of cooling during an extreme summer heat warning with vulnerable occupants. If you smell gas or suspect carbon monoxide, evacuate the building immediately and contact emergency authorities before calling for service.

What should I do while waiting for an emergency heating technician?

If your furnace stops working in freezing weather, first verify that your thermostat is set to heat with the temperature set above room temperature, confirm your electrical panel breaker has not tripped, and check that your exterior furnace exhaust vents are not choked with snow or ice. If those checks do not resolve the problem, call our emergency line immediately. To retain warmth and protect plumbing while waiting for dispatch, close interior doors, open under sink cabinet doors to keep water pipes warm, and avoid using dangerous open flames or outdoor heaters indoors.

How quickly does JUJCO dispatch for emergency HVAC calls in Alberta?

Our 24/7 emergency service provides rapid dispatch across Alberta. When you call our emergency number, our on call team prioritizes life safety calls, such as homes without heat in subzero temperatures or properties with urgent gas or carbon monoxide concerns. We provide an estimated arrival window immediately over the phone and dispatch certified technicians in fully equipped service vehicles prepared to complete immediate repairs.

Can most heating and cooling emergencies be repaired on the first visit?

Yes. Our certified technicians arrive in service vehicles stocked with essential diagnostic tools and universal replacement parts, including hot surface igniters, flame sensors, run capacitors, blower fan motors, safety switches, and electronic control boards. In the rare event that a proprietary manufacturer part must be ordered, we secure your heating equipment, provide temporary heating solutions when needed, and prioritize daytime installation as soon as suppliers open.
