Night calls are why the second number exists

The office line is for quotes and weekday repairs. The emergency line is for no-heat at −28°C, a CO alarm, a gas smell, or no cooling during a heat warning. We keep common ignitors, capacitors, and boards on the truck so a night visit can actually end with heat or cooling, not just a diagnosis.

If a part has to come in, we still make the house livable - temporary heat, a safe shutdown, or a next-morning first slot. We do not leave you guessing what happens at 8 a.m.

Questions we actually get

What counts as an emergency?

No heat in freezing weather, suspected gas or CO, flooding from a boiler or humidifier, or no cooling when health is at risk. A noisy blower at 2 a.m. can usually wait.

Do you charge more after hours?

After-hours dispatch costs more than a Tuesday morning slot. We tell you that on the phone before we roll.

What if you cannot fix it tonight?

We make it safe and livable, then book the first parts window. You will not be left without a plan.

Which number do I save?

Office (780) 982-2577 for booking. Emergency (780) 982-3377 for after-hours.
