Repair · Cooling

Cooling repair without the guesswork

The most common July call is a condenser that hums and blows warm. In many cases it is a capacitor, contactor, or fan motor we can change from the truck. We still measure amp draw so a dying compressor does not get a Band-Aid.

We do not 'top up' refrigerant on a leak and walk away. If the charge is low we look for the leak, tell you the options, and only weigh in what the system is supposed to hold after the leak is handled.

Questions we actually get

Why is there ice on my AC?

Usually airflow or a low charge. Running it that way can wreck the compressor. Turn it off and call.

Can you recharge R-22?

R-22 is a legacy refrigerant. We will tell you the honest cost of a leak repair versus a new system.

How often should AC be serviced?

Once a year in spring is enough for most Canadian homes. Filters are on you between visits.

Do you repair window units?

No. Central systems, coils, and condensers only.
